# BMW's Global Journey: 2023-2024 E-Commerce & Sales Analytics Dashboard

An executive-level Power BI dashboard analyzing BMW's global sales performance, vehicle distribution, customer demographics, and fuel-type transitions from 2023 to 2024.

## Executive Summary & Key Insights

* **Total Sales Performance:** Reached **$191M Total Sales** in 2024 with a **+6.46% YoY Growth** compared to 2023 ($179M).
* **Fuel Type & Sustainability:** Tracks regional distribution across Diesel, Electric, Hybrid, and Petrol engines, highlighting market transition toward Electric and Hybrid vehicles.
* **Engine Class Market Share:** Analyzes market share categorized into *Large Engine* (26K units), *Electric* (12K units), *Medium Engine*, and *Small Engine*.
* **Customer Demographics:** Average customer age identified at **40.06 years** with an average car retention age of **2.00 years**.

---

## Technical Implementation & Power BI Features

* **Data Modeling:** Multi-dimensional schema linking vehicle models, sales transactions, regional geometries, and fuel classifications.
* **Advanced DAX Measures:**
  * Dynamic **Year-over-Year (YoY%)** growth measures using Time Intelligence functions (`SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR`).
  * Dynamic Total Sales, Average Vehicle Price, and Target Variance Gauges.
* **Data Transformation (Power Query):** Cleansed raw sales records, unpivoted attributes, handled data types, and prepared geographical parameters.
* **UI/UX Design:** Dark-themed executive layout prioritizing scannability, interactive slicers, cross-filtering, and customized map visuals (Microsoft Azure Maps).

---

## How to View

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `Simay_Final_DB.pbix` file using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Interact with regional filters, model categories, and year slicers to drill down into metrics.
